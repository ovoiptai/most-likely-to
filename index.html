How to play

  1. Everyone gets the same question on the phone — except the impostors, who secretly get a totally different one.
  2. Each player holds the card to peek, then writes down the name of whoever fits their question best.
  3. Nobody knows if they're the impostor. Everyone thinks their question is the real one.
  4. When everyone has answered, the real question is revealed to the room — and the impostors realise theirs was different.
  5. Go around and say who you picked and why. The impostors now have to justify an answer to a question they never saw.
  6. Then vote. Name an impostor and they're caught. Name an innocent and they're out of the game — and you guess again from whoever's left.
  7. Catch every impostor and the crew wins. Eject too many innocents and the impostors win.
  8. At the end you find out what the impostors were actually answering.
